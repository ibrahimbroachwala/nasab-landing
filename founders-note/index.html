A note from the founder

Why I'm building Nasab


I am a new father. My daughter just turned two, and somewhere between all the sleepless nights and the tiny moments that make it all worth it, I found myself thinking a lot about stories.

My own father had an amazing childhood. He also has a few horror stories from those years, the kind that make you laugh nervously every single time he tells them. I have heard these stories so many times growing up that they almost feel like my own memories. And then one day, I stopped and wondered. Will my daughter ever get to hear these stories the way I did? Will she know her grandfather through his own voice and his own way of telling a joke, or will he just be a name she reads somewhere one day?

That thought stayed with me.

Not long after, my brother sent me something that caught me completely off guard. A family tree that went all the way back to our great great great grandfather. My uncle and my father had built it years ago, back when I was still in school. They made the whole thing on PowerPoint, and it took them several days of work to put together. My brother had found that very file sitting quietly in an old email from 2010, almost forgotten, tucked away for years, and sent it to me the moment I first told him about the idea for a family tree app.

Around the same time, I remembered something else my father used to do. Every time a family member was born, or a couple celebrated an anniversary, he would add the date to an Excel sheet, with my brother's help, and print it out and stick the printout inside his cupboard. He would refer to that page and wish people in the family on their birthdays and anniversaries. It was his own quiet way of making sure no one in the family was ever forgotten.

A tree buried in an old PowerPoint file. A list of dates stuck inside a cupboard. Stories that live only in the memory of whoever heard them first. Put together, it made something very clear to me. Our families carry so much history and love, but we rarely have one real place to keep it. Nothing that survives the years, or the people, or whichever device it happened to be saved on.

That is why I started building Nasab.

I wanted to take that old PowerPoint file and finally give it a proper home. A place where my brother, my uncle, my father, and everyone who comes after me could keep adding to the same family tree, without it ever getting buried in an inbox again. A shared space where every member of the family, young or old, could add their own photos, their own voice notes, and their own memories.

Nasab brings all of this together in one place. A family tree that keeps growing with you, simple enough that adding a new member takes just a few seconds. Memory albums to hold your photos and voices safely for years to come. Reminders for birthdays and anniversaries, so no one needs to keep a paper list in a cupboard anymore. One space where you can look back and actually see how your family has grown and changed over time.

I hope Nasab becomes the place where your family's stories get to live on, the same way I hope my daughter grows up hearing her grandfather's stories in his own words, whenever she wants, for as long as she wants.

I hope you enjoy using it as much as I enjoy building it.

Nasab has been built with a lot of passion and love, and there is still so much more I want to add to it. If you have any feedback, ideas, or even just a story of your own you would like to share, I would genuinely love to hear from you at tappstudio.in@gmail.com.

With love,

Ibrahim BroachwalaFounder, Nasab
