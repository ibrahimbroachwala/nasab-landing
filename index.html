What Nasab does

One place for your family's whole story

I

A family tree that keeps growing

Unlimited generations, parents, spouses, and children mapped as your family actually looks, with vertical, horizontal, and radial views. For families who use patronymic naming, Nasab renders it correctly throughout.

II

A timeline that writes itself

Births, marriages, and deaths are placed on a chronological timeline automatically as you add them, with no manual event logging and no upkeep. Just a quiet record of when everything happened.

III

Albums bound like keepsakes

Photos and videos, captioned and tagged to the people in them, organized into themed albums. Some memories are for everyone; others, you choose who sees.

IV

A voice, not just a memory

Record and preserve voice notes from the people in your family, so the way they sounded, and the way they told a story, is never lost to time.

V

A gentle nudge, never a chore

Nasab notices when a branch of your family is missing (a parent not yet added, a voice not yet recorded) and quietly nudges you to fill it in. It also keeps an eye on what's coming: birthdays and anniversaries, so none slip by unnoticed.

Questions

Before you join

Who can see our family's memories?

Only the people you invite into your family's Nasab. Every memory is stored securely, and albums can be shared with everyone in the family or kept visible to just the people you choose.

What happens to our archive if we cancel Heritage?

Your tree and memories stay intact. On Roots, some Heritage-only features (voice notes, video, radial view, unlimited albums) become unavailable until you upgrade again, but nothing is deleted.

Why not just use a family WhatsApp group or Google Photos?

A WhatsApp group buries photos under daily chatter and they're hard to find again months later. Google Photos albums solve the "everyone can add" problem, but the photos still sit in a flat album. There's no sense of who's related to whom, no timeline that places a photo in the story of someone's life, and no way to record a voice memory as its own thing rather than a video clip. Nasab starts from your family tree: every memory is tagged to the people in it, placed automatically on a timeline of births, marriages, and milestones, and easy to find years later by simply looking up the person. It's built to be your family's structured history, not another folder of photos.

How is this different from ancestry or genealogy apps?

Nasab isn't built for searching archival records or tracing distant ancestry. It's built for the family you already have: the tree you're related to, the stories still worth telling, the voices still worth recording.

Does everyone in my family need their own subscription?

No, one subscription covers everyone you invite. They join, view, and contribute for free.

Will this work for relatives who aren't comfortable with technology?

Joining takes one invite code. Once someone's in, adding a memory is just a photo, a voice note, or a birthday, with no manual event logging and no upkeep.

What's the difference between Nasab Roots and Nasab Heritage?

The tree itself is never limited on either plan: every generation, unlimited members. Heritage adds more shared storage, higher upload quality, voice notes, video memories, the radial tree view, and unlimited albums.

How do the gentle nudges work?

Nasab notices when a branch is incomplete, like a parent not yet added or a voice not yet recorded, and quietly nudges you to fill it in. It also surfaces upcoming birthdays and anniversaries so none slip by.

Can more than one person add to the tree?

Yes. Everyone you invite claims their own place in the tree and adds their own memories; one owner keeps things tidy, everyone else contributes as a member.
