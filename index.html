Questions

Before you join

Why Nasab

Why not just use a family WhatsApp group or Google Photos?

A WhatsApp group buries photos under daily chatter, so they're hard to find later. Google Photos lets everyone add photos, but they all sit in one flat album with no sense of who's related to whom. Nasab starts from your family tree instead. Every memory is tagged to the people in it and placed automatically on a timeline of births, marriages, and milestones, so you can find it years later just by looking up the person. It's your family's structured history, not another folder of photos.

How is this different from ancestry or genealogy apps?

Nasab isn't for searching old records or tracing distant ancestors. It's for the family you already have: the people you're close to, the stories still worth telling, and the voices still worth recording.

Getting your family started

Will this work for relatives who aren't comfortable with technology?

Joining takes one tap on an invite link, nothing to type or copy. If the app isn't installed yet, the link sends them to the App Store or Play Store, and their invite is waiting the moment they open the app for the first time. After that, adding a memory is just a photo, a voice note, or a birthday. No manual logging, no upkeep.

What if a relative gets the invite link but doesn't have the app yet?

They just tap it. The link recognizes their phone, sends them to install Nasab from the App Store or Play Store, and remembers who invited them. Their invite is already filled in the first time they open the app. No code to find or re-enter, even after the install.

The family tree

Can more than one person add to the tree?

Yes. Everyone you invite gets their own place in the tree and adds their own memories. By default, any member can add or edit anyone else too. An Owner can turn that off and appoint specific Editors instead. Everyone still keeps the ability to edit their own close family: parents, spouse, siblings, and children.

What can a Nasab Editor do?

An Editor is a trusted helper the family Owner appoints. It's never a role you can give yourself. Editors can add or edit any member and relationship in the tree, and review requests to join through marriage alongside the Owner. It's for families who'd rather keep tree edits in a few trusted hands instead of leaving them open to everyone.

Can anyone add anyone to the tree?

Not without oversight. When someone joins the tree through marriage and starts adding their own relatives, those additions are held for the family Owner, or an appointed Editor, to approve before they become visible. That way, no one's story gets added to your family's archive without your say.

Can I include family members who've passed away?

Yes. Mark a member as deceased, and their place in the tree, their timeline, their memories, and any voice notes they left behind stay exactly where they are, visible to the family for good.

Does the family tree support multiple or past spouses?

Yes. Adding a second wife to a husband is simple: just add another spouse from the husband's profile. Adding a second husband to a wife works a little differently, since Nasab keeps one marriage active at a time on the wife's side: mark the previous husband as a past marriage first by long pressing his avatar on the wife's profile, then add the new spouse as usual.

Memories & the archive

How do the gentle nudges work?

Nasab notices when something's missing, like a parent not yet added or a voice not yet recorded, and gently reminds you to fill it in, so your family's story stays complete.

Will I be reminded about birthdays and anniversaries?

Yes. Upcoming birthdays and anniversaries for everyone in the family show right on your home screen, and you also get a push notification on the day itself, so none slip by.

Can I add old, printed photos to our archive?

Yes. Nasab can scan a physical photo straight into the archive, straightening and cropping it for you. It becomes a normal memory alongside everything else, tagged to the people in it.

Can I export our family tree?

Yes. From the tree screen, export a printable, poster-style PDF of your tree, or a GEDCOM file compatible with other genealogy tools like Ancestry, FamilySearch, and MyHeritage. Share either one straight from your phone.

Language & the Hijri calendar

Can I use Nasab in Arabic?

Yes. Open Settings, then Language, and choose العربية. The whole app switches to Arabic straight away, with no restart, and the layout flips to right-to-left. Nasab opens in English the first time; you can switch back and forth whenever you like, and your choice is remembered on that device.

What does the Hijri Dates switch do?

When you turn on Hijri Dates in Family Settings, every date in the app, births, marriages, memories, and the timeline, shows its Islamic-calendar equivalent under the ordinary date. Dates are always stored as regular Gregorian dates; the Hijri version is only shown alongside. It's a per-device preference: turning it on or off changes only your own phone, not what other family members see.

Which Hijri calendar does Nasab use?

You can choose between two. Misri (Fatimid) is a fixed tabular calendar, calculated the same way every year with no moon-sighting adjustments, and is the one Dawoodi Bohra communities follow. Umm al-Qura is Saudi Arabia's official calendar. Pick the one your family uses under Family Settings, then Hijri Calendar.

Who picks the Hijri calendar type for our family?

That's a family-wide setting, and only the family Owner can change it, so everyone sees Hijri dates in the same system. Other members can see which calendar is selected but can't change it. It's separate from the Hijri Dates on/off switch, which each person controls for their own device.

Why does an older date sometimes show no Hijri equivalent?

The Umm al-Qura calendar is only defined for roughly 1937 to 2077. For dates outside that window, an ancestor born in the 1800s, say, Nasab leaves the Hijri line off rather than showing an unreliable conversion. The Misri (Fatimid) calendar has no such limit and works for any year.

Privacy & access

Who can see our family's memories?

Only the people you invite to your family's Nasab. Every memory is stored securely. You can share an album with the whole family, or keep it visible to just the people you choose.

What happens if someone wants to leave the family archive?

They can leave at any time, and nothing they've contributed is deleted. Their memories, comments, and profile stay part of the family's story. An Owner can't leave without deleting the family outright, since there's no way to transfer ownership yet.

Trial & subscription

Does everyone in my family need their own subscription?

No, one Heritage subscription covers everyone you invite. They join, view, and contribute under it at no extra cost to them.

How does the 14-day free trial work?

Every family gets a 14-day free trial of Nasab Heritage as soon as they sign up: full access to the tree, timeline, memory albums, and voice notes from day one. The family Owner can subscribe at any point during or after the trial to keep that access going.

What happens when the trial ends, or if we cancel later?

Nothing in your archive is touched. Your tree, memories, and voice notes stay exactly as they are. But without an active Heritage subscription, the app locks for the Owner and every invited member until the Owner subscribes again.
