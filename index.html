Nasab isn't built for searching archival records or tracing distant ancestry. It's built for the family you already have — the tree you're related to, the stories still worth telling, the voices still worth recording. Add a parent, a memory, a birthday, and the picture grows a little more complete.

What Nasab does

Everything shipped, nothing promised

A family tree that keeps growing

Unlimited generations, parents, spouses, and children mapped as your family actually looks — with vertical, horizontal, and radial views. For families who use patronymic naming, Nasab renders it correctly throughout.

A timeline that writes itself

Births, marriages, and deaths are placed on a chronological timeline automatically as you add them — no manual event logging, no upkeep. Just a quiet record of when everything happened.

Memories, in every form they take

Photos, videos, and voice notes — captioned, tagged to the people in them, and organized into themed albums bound like keepsakes. Some memories are for everyone; others, you choose who sees.

A gentle nudge, never a chore

Nasab notices when a branch of your family is missing — a parent not yet added, a voice not yet recorded — and quietly nudges you to fill it in. It also keeps an eye on what's coming: birthdays and anniversaries, so none slip by unnoticed.

Built together, not alone

Invite your family with a single code — everyone claims their own place in the tree, adds their own memories, and the archive grows as a family, not a solo project. One owner keeps things tidy; everyone else contributes as a member.
